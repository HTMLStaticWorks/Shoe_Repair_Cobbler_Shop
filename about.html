Master Techniques

The Care Taken During Every Repair

A proper boot resole is not just glued together. We carefully strip the old worn outsole, inspect the gemming and cork filler, replace degraded cork with fresh natural cork paste, and re-stitch through the original welt holes to preserve upper integrity.

Finally, soles are edge-trimmed on specialized finisher wheels and hand-buffed with carnauba wax to achieve factory perfection.
