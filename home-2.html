Footwear Longevity

Essential Shoe Care Tips

Simple habits can double the lifespan of high-quality leather shoes between cobbler resolings.

  • ✓ Condition Leather Regularly: Apply natural cream conditioner every 4-6 weeks to prevent cracking.
  • ✓ Use Cedar Shoe Trees: Insert cedar trees immediately after wearing to absorb moisture and preserve shape.
  • ✓ Avoid Direct Heat: Never dry wet leather near radiators or heaters, which causes stiffening and dry rot.
  • ✓ Rotate Frequently Worn Shoes: Allow at least 24 hours of rest between wears for leather to fully dry.
